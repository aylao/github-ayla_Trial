# Business Plan

> The full operational business plan lives in `BUSINESS_PLAN.md` at the repo root.
> This note summarizes and links the key sections.

## Sections in the full plan

- **What this is** — the core product vision
- **Phase timeline** — legal → infra → founding creators → soft launch → public launch
- **Phase 1: Legal setup** — Wyoming LLC, EIN, Mercury, DMCA, lawyer review
- **Phase 2: Tech setup** — full stack with costs, build backlog, platform integrations
- **Phase 3: Founding Creator outreach** — outreach script, where to find them, what to say
- **Monetization** — revenue streams, projections to month 12
- **Marketing & advertising** — phase-by-phase plan, demographic targeting
- **Key risks** — and mitigations for each
- **Decision log** — record of decisions made and why
- **Subscriptions checklist** — every account/service to set up with costs

## Key numbers

- **Launch cost:** ~$175 one-time + $22/mo recurring
- **Break-even:** 7 Pro subscribers
- **Target month 12 MRR:** $20,000
- **Creator split:** 80% / 20%
