# Naming & Brand

## App name: Forkable

**Why this name:**
- Describes the core action instantly (fork an app)
- Short, memorable, works as a verb ("just forkable it")
- Strong domain potential: forkable.dev / forkable.app
- Clean social handles: @forkable on Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok

**Previous placeholder:** AppForge (still in some parts of the codebase — run `/rename-app AppForge Forkable` to clean up)

## Brand identity notes

**Vibe:** Approachable but capable. For creative builders, not enterprise developers.

**Color:** Violet/purple primary (already in codebase — `violet-600`) + Amber accent for Pro/founding creator.

**Tone of voice:**
- Direct, not corporate
- Builder-to-builder (not platform-to-user)
- Celebrates creativity and "shipping" culture
- Never condescending to non-coders
