# Marketing & Advertising

## Core demographic

**"Techy artists"** — creative people who have ideas and are discovering they can build with vibe coding tools. Musicians, designers, illustrators, writers, non-technical founders. They share what they make online. They're underserved by GitHub culture.

This is the wedge. They're high-signal, social, and not well-targeted by existing developer tools marketing.

## Phase 1: Pre-launch (Weeks 1–8) — $0

### Daily Twitter/X posting — the most important habit
- One app per day with a GIF or screenshot
- Tag the creator — they retweet to their audience
- Format that works: "This [type] app was built in [tool] and forked [X] times. Here's why it's great: [thread]"

### Discord seeding
- Lovable Discord
- Bolt Discord  
- Replit Discord
- Cursor Discord
- Participate genuinely first. Don't spam.

### Build in public
- "Building Forkable, day [N]" posts perform well with the indie maker audience
- Share honest numbers once they exist

### Founding Creator outreach (see [[Business Plan]] for script)
- Target: 50 DMs to active vibe coders on Twitter
- Goal: 20 real apps before soft launch

## Phase 2: Launch (Months 1–3) — $200–500/mo

### Twitter/X promoted posts
- Target followers of: @levelsio, Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Cursor official accounts
- Budget: $100–200/mo

### Reddit (organic first)
- r/SideProject, r/ChatGPT, r/artificial, r/webdev
- Post when hitting milestones, not promotional posts
- No paid Reddit until month 6

### Product Hunt launch
- Prepare 3 weeks in advance
- Get founding creators to upvote and comment on launch day
- Aim for top 5 of the day
- This is a one-shot opportunity — don't waste it by launching before content is ready

### Email newsletter
- "App of the week" — one email per week
- Start collecting emails from day one (waitlist form exists)
- This list is your most reliable long-term channel

## Phase 3: Growth (Months 3–6) — $500–1,500/mo

### TikTok / Instagram Reels
- "I forked this app and added [feature] in 10 minutes" format
- Highest reach for the techy-artist demographic
- Either do it yourself (if comfortable on camera) or hire part-time video editor ($200–400/mo)

### Twitter/X scale
- Increase promoted posts to $300–500/mo targeting what worked in phase 2

### Creator partnerships
- Pay 2–3 founding creators with large audiences ($100–300 each) to post when they submit a new app

## Paid targeting audiences

For when you run paid ads:
- Followers of Canva, Figma, Adobe (creatives with tech curiosity)
- Followers of Lovable, Bolt, Replit official accounts
- Followers of "build in public" Twitter accounts
- Interests: music production, digital art, independent creator tools, AI tools

## Metrics to track

- Weekly new app submissions
- Weekly new accounts created
- Fork click rate (forks / app views)
- Pro conversion rate (Pro signups / total accounts)
- Email list growth
- Twitter follower growth
