# Target Audience

> [!TIP] The best early adopter isn't the most technical person — it's the "techy artist": creative-minded, curious about AI, frustrated that they can't build the exact tool they imagine.

---

## Primary persona — The Techy Artist

**Who:** Designers, musicians, writers, photographers, teachers, and other creatives who use AI tools daily and want more but can't code from scratch.

**Demographics:**
- Age: 24–40
- Job: Creative professional, educator, freelancer, content creator
- Tools they already use: Figma, Notion, Canva, Midjourney, ChatGPT, Spotify
- Income: $40–90k/yr (budget-conscious, but pays for tools that save time)

**Frustrations:**
- "I have a specific idea but can't find an app that does exactly what I need"
- "I tried to describe my idea to ChatGPT but it just gave me code I can't use"
- "I want to customize this app but don't know where to start"

**What Forkable gives them:**
- Browse apps built by people with similar creative needs
- One-click fork into a vibe coding tool they already use (Lovable, Bolt)
- A low-risk way to "remix" an app without starting from zero

**How to reach them:**
- Twitter/X (creative + tech crossover accounts)
- Instagram and TikTok (short demos of forking an app)
- YouTube tutorials ("I forked this app and customized it in 20 min")
- Communities: r/SideProject, Midjourney Discord, Notion's community, design Twitter

---

## Secondary persona — The Active Vibe Coder

**Who:** Builders who actively use Lovable, Bolt, Cursor, Replit, v0. They ship fast and often. They want discovery and distribution.

**Demographics:**
- Age: 22–45
- Background: Developer, product manager, designer who learned to code with AI
- Tools: Lovable, Bolt.new, Cursor, Replit, Vercel, Supabase
- Income: $60–150k/yr (pays for tools, evaluates ROI quickly)

**Frustrations:**
- "I build great stuff but no one discovers it"
- "I share on Twitter but the engagement fades fast"
- "I don't have a monetization story for my side apps"

**What Forkable gives them:**
- A permanent home for their apps beyond a tweet
- Discovery via the Forkable browse page
- Actual revenue: paid forks with 80% payout

**How to reach them:**
- Lovable, Bolt, Cursor community Discords
- "Shipped something on Forkable" Twitter posts
- Replit's community showcase

---

## Tertiary persona — The Busy Professional

**Who:** Non-technical professionals (HR managers, marketers, accountants, therapists) who've heard about AI tools and want ready-to-go solutions.

**Demographics:**
- Age: 30–55
- Job: Middle management, small business owner, freelance professional
- Not a builder — wants to use, not fork

**Frustrations:**
- "I don't have time to learn to code, even with AI"
- "I just want an app that does X without hiring a developer"

**What Forkable gives them:**
- Browse and find ready-to-use AI apps in their category
- Paid forks: they pay once and get a working app they can use
- Clear descriptions with live previews so they know what they're getting

**How to reach them:**
- LinkedIn sponsored posts
- "AI tools for [profession]" SEO content
- Product Hunt (they browse PH for tools)

---

## What unites all three

1. They're frustrated by the gap between "I have an idea" and "I have a working app"
2. They're already using AI tools — they just want better discovery
3. They respond to **seeing before buying** (live preview is the killer feature)
4. They share things they love (virality potential)
