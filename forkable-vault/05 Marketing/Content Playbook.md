# Content Playbook

> [!NOTE] Everything here assumes a solo founder with limited time. Pick 1–2 formats and do them consistently rather than doing all of them sporadically.

---

## Top-priority formats

### 1. "App of the Week" Twitter thread
**Cadence:** Weekly (Tuesdays work well — lower noise day)
**Time to produce:** 20–30 min
**Template:**

```
🔀 App of the Week on [Platform name]:

[App name] by @[creator]

What it does: [1 sentence]
Who it's for: [techy artist persona]
Fork it in: [platform icons]

[Screenshot]

Here's why I love it 🧵
```

Thread continues with 3–5 tweets about what makes the app interesting, a quote from the creator, and a link to the Forkable listing.

---

### 2. Demo video (60 seconds)
**Cadence:** 2x per month
**Platforms:** Twitter/X, TikTok, Instagram Reels, LinkedIn
**Format:** Screen recording, no face required
**Script structure:**
1. (0–5s) "Here's a problem" — show the frustrating thing
2. (5–25s) "Here's the app on Forkable" — browse to it, click preview
3. (25–50s) "Watch me fork it" — click fork button, Replit opens with the code
4. (50–60s) "Now I can customize it" — end on the payoff

---

### 3. "Built with [Platform]" posts
**Cadence:** Reactive (when a Founding Creator ships something)
**Template:**

```
@[creator] just dropped a [type] app on [Platform name] built with [Lovable/Bolt/etc]

It does [X]. Anyone making [Y content] should fork this.

🔗 [link]
```

This drives traffic to the creator's profile and gives them a reason to share the post.

---

### 4. Monthly roundup email
**Cadence:** Monthly
**Tool:** Resend (free tier)
**Content:**
- 5 new apps worth forking
- Trending tags this month
- 1 featured creator spotlight
- Platform news (new integrations, features)

**Subject line formats that work:**
- "5 apps to fork this weekend"
- "The best thing built on [Platform name] in June"
- "New: [Feature] is live"

## Hashtags and communities

**Hashtags to use:**
- `#vibecoding` `#buildinpublic` `#nocode` `#aitools` `#indiedev`
- `#lovable` `#boltdotnew` `#replit` (platform-specific threads)

**Communities to post in:**
- r/SideProject (build-in-public posts)
- r/nocode (app discovery)
- r/artificial (AI tool recommendations)
- Lovable Discord `#showcase`
- Bolt.new Discord `#show-and-tell`
- Buildspace alumni network
- Indie Hackers (milestone posts)

---

## What NOT to do

- Don't post generic "check out my platform" tweets
- Don't spam DMs to people who didn't opt in
- Don't post the same content on every platform simultaneously (adapt format)
- Don't chase virality — consistency beats one-off spikes
