# Wyoming LLC Setup

> [!NOTE] You live in New York but should form in Wyoming. See [[NY State Notes]] for why.

## Step 1 — Form the LLC

**Service:** Northwest Registered Agent — northwestregisteredagent.com
**Cost:** ~$100 Wyoming state filing fee + $39/yr registered agent fee
**Time:** 3–5 business days

- [ ] Go to northwestregisteredagent.com
- [ ] Choose "Form an LLC" → Wyoming
- [ ] LLC name: **Forkable LLC** (or a holding name — your call)
- [ ] Pay and submit
- [ ] Download Articles of Organization when approved

> [!TIP] Northwest's registered agent service ($39/yr) handles the address requirement — you don't need a Wyoming address yourself.

---

## Step 2 — Get an EIN

**Where:** IRS.gov → Apply for EIN
**Cost:** Free
**Time:** 10 minutes, same day

- [ ] Go to IRS.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
- [ ] Entity type: LLC
- [ ] Reason: Started a new business
- [ ] Write down the EIN immediately — it's shown once

---

## Step 3 — Open Mercury bank account

**Where:** mercury.com
**Cost:** Free, no minimum balance
**Time:** 1–3 business days to approve

- [ ] Sign up at mercury.com
- [ ] Have ready: LLC name, EIN, Articles of Organization, your ID
- [ ] Choose the free tier
- [ ] This is the account Stripe payouts will go to

---

## Step 4 — Operating Agreement (optional but good)

Wyoming doesn't require a formal operating agreement but it's good practice. Northwest provides a simple template. Since this is a single-member LLC, it's a 1-page document.

- [ ] Download template from Northwest
- [ ] Sign and date it
- [ ] Store it with your LLC documents

---

## Documents to keep

Once formed, keep these somewhere safe (1Password, Google Drive):
- Articles of Organization
- EIN confirmation letter
- Operating Agreement
- Annual report receipts
