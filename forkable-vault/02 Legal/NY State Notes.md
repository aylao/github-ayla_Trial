# NY State Notes

*You live in New York. Here's how that affects Forkable specifically.*

---

## Why NOT to form an NY LLC

New York requires LLCs to publish a notice in two local newspapers for 6 consecutive weeks after formation. The cost varies by county:

| County | Approximate cost |
|--------|-----------------|
| New York (Manhattan) | $1,500–$2,000 |
| Kings (Brooklyn) | $1,200–$1,800 |
| Queens | $1,000–$1,500 |
| Bronx | $800–$1,200 |
| Outside NYC | $300–$600 |

This is a pure bureaucratic tax with no benefit. Wyoming has no such requirement.

**You can live in NY and operate a Wyoming LLC.** This is completely legal and extremely common among NY founders. Your Wyoming registered agent handles the address requirement.

---

## NY Sales Tax on SaaS

New York charges sales tax on certain digital products and SaaS subscriptions above specific revenue thresholds.

> [!WARNING] This is not a launch blocker, but don't ignore it once revenue starts.

**What to do:**
- Tell your accountant about Forkable on day one
- When revenue starts, set up TaxJar or Avalara to calculate NY sales tax automatically
- Stripe Tax can handle this once you're integrated with Stripe

**Rough threshold:** Once you have meaningful NY customers paying subscriptions, consult your accountant. Don't guess.

---

## NY Personal Income Tax

You'll pay NY state income tax on your share of LLC profits. This is expected — nothing special about Forkable changes this.

**Practical tips:**
- Keep business and personal finances completely separate (Mercury bank account helps)
- Set aside ~30% of profit for taxes (federal + NY state)
- Use accounting software from the start (Wave is free, QuickBooks is $30/mo)

---

## What NY does NOT affect

- DMCA agent registration (federal, not state)
- Wyoming LLC formation and annual maintenance
- Stripe/payment processing
- Your ability to operate a fully national/international product
