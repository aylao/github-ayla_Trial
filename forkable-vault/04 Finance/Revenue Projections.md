## What drives growth

1. **Founding creator content** — each founding creator who posts to their audience = free traffic spike
2. **Product Hunt launch** — one-shot, high-ceiling traffic event
3. **Fork virality** — every paid fork is a user who may submit their own apps
4. **SEO** — "fork [type] app" is a real search pattern. Each app detail page can rank.
5. **Sponsored placements** — once established, brands pay for category visibility

---

## Assumptions

- 5% of visitors create accounts
- 10% of free accounts upgrade to Pro
- Average paid fork price: $9
- Average forks per paid app per month: 15
- Churn rate: 5%/mo (industry standard for early-stage SaaS)
